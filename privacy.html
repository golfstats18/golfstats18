Privacy Policy

Golf Stats 18  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Who We Are

Golf Stats 18 is a golf performance tracking application operated by Golf Stats 18 (golfstats18@gmail.com). We are based in Scotland, United Kingdom.

What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the service:

Account data: Your email address and encrypted password, used to create and manage your account.

Golf round data: Scores, fairway results, green in regulation results, putts, course names, handicap, and hole configurations that you enter into the app.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, payment details, or any other personal information.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with the Golf Stats 18 service — storing your rounds and showing you your statistics. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app.

Who We Share Your Data With

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

Supabase (supabase.com) — our database and authentication provider. Your account and round data is stored securely on Supabase servers in the EU. Supabase is GDPR compliant.

Netlify (netlify.com) — our hosting provider. Netlify serves the app files but does not have access to your personal data or round data.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third parties.

Data Security

Your password is encrypted and never stored in plain text. Your round data is protected by row-level security — only you can access your own rounds. All data is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at golfstats18@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

Cookies

Golf Stats 18 does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We use a session token stored in your browser to keep you logged in — this is essential for the app to function.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy questions or data requests, contact us at golfstats18@gmail.com.
